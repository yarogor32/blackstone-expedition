# Combat SFX prompts

## Morale breakdown — 4 seconds

Create a 4-second cinematic psychological breakdown sound effect for a dark gothic science-fiction RPG. A sudden inward rush, followed by a deep hollow impact and a piercing, fragile glass-like ringing inside the mind. Layer faint overlapping wordless whispers and strained dissonant tones, suggesting resolve shattering and overwhelming dread. Intense but restrained enough for repeated gameplay. The sound swells quickly during the first 0.3 seconds, trembles and pulses through the middle, then dissolves into a smooth eerie reverb tail. No intelligible speech, no character scream, no music, no drums, no abrupt cutoff. A single isolated game sound effect with a clean beginning and complete decay.

## Zoanthrope psychic attack — 3 seconds

Create a 3-second alien psychic attack sound effect for a floating insectoid creature in a dark gothic science-fiction RPG. Start with a brief rising pressure whine and a throbbing organic resonance. At 0.2 seconds, release a sharp psychic rupture: a dense bass pulse beneath a tearing, glassy electrical shriek, as if invisible force is striking directly inside someone's mind. Follow with unstable, warbling psychic echoes and a smooth fading reverb tail. Alien, menacing and supernatural. No gunshot, no mechanical weapon, no physical explosion, no human voice, no music, no abrupt cutoff. One distinct attack, not a repeated loop.

# Enemy abilities: initial balance

- Hormagaunt: 40% Rending Shove / 60% Scything Talons. Both 2–4 HP, 85% accuracy, 5% critical chance. Successful nonlethal shove moves the frontmost living hero back one rank, swapping with a living occupant. Rank 4 cannot be pushed farther. Miss does not move anyone.
- Termagant: Fleshborer Volley targets up to two living heroes at the rear. Each target rolls independently: 2–3 HP, 80% accuracy, 5% critical chance. With one survivor it fires once.
- Zoanthrope: Psychic Assault targets the living hero with lowest morale. 14–20 base morale loss, 85% accuracy, 8% critical chance, no HP damage. Existing critical and breakdown rules apply: critical max damage ×1.5 plus 10 critical morale loss; reaching zero triggers breakdown if newly depleted/unafflicted. Morale loss is capped by available morale.

These are gameplay balance defaults, not lore statistics. New sound files are not connected until generated audio is provided.
